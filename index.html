Technical Events

01

Paper Presentation

1–3 Members  ·  10 min + Q&A

Present your research or innovative idea before a panel of eminent judges. Judged on clarity, originality, and depth. A platform to turn your ideas into recognition and sharpen your ability to communicate technically under pressure.

Abstract requiredAny domainCertificates for all

Technical Quiz

Team of 2  ·  1.5 Hours

A high-octane knowledge battle covering programming fundamentals, emerging technologies, AI, cybersecurity, and digital innovations. Multiple rounds — prelims, rapid-fire, and buzzer — each more intense than the last.

Buzzer roundsRapid-firePrelims + Finals

Prompt Battle

Individual / Pair  ·  45 Min

The AI era is here — and those who command it, lead it. Craft the most effective, creative, and precise prompts to generate superior AI outputs. Judged on prompt quality, result produced, and creativity of approach.

AI tools providedCreativity judgedNew-age format

Individual  ·  1 Hour

Given a partial piece of code — complete it correctly and efficiently to produce the expected output. Tests your logic, syntax, debugging instinct and problem-solving under real time pressure. Every line counts.

Any languageLogic + syntaxTime-pressured
